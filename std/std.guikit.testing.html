    CHECKING A WIDGET IN A UI TEST (ui_test.sharp has the overview)

    should_exist()          there is such a widget on the screen
should_not_exist()      there is none
should_be_shown()       it is there and can be seen: not hidden, not scrolled out of sight
should_be_hidden()      it cannot be seen (or is not there at all)
    should_be_enabled()     it can be used
should_be_disabled()    it is greyed out
should_be_on()          a checkbox is ticked, a switch on, a radio or a chip the chosen one
should_be_off()
should_be_selected()    the same as should_be_on(): for a radio, a chip
should_be_focused()     it has the keyboard
    should_say("words")     it shows exactly these words (a text field: this is typed in it)
should_contain("part")  these words are somewhere in what it shows
should_have_value(0.5)  a slider or a progress bar stands at this number

    A check that does not hold fails the test THERE, like expect(...) of std.testing, and the message ends with the screen as text. One that holds answers the same ui_node, so more can follow:

    on_tag("total").should_be_shown().should_say("Count: 1");

    TOASTS AND THE TOOLTIP are not widgets: the window paints them over everything itself. on_text(...) does not find them; these do:

    should_show_toast("Saved")      a toast with exactly these words is up
should_show_no_toast()          none is (after advance(...) let its time pass)
should_show_tooltip("Delete")   the tooltip with these words is up (hover() a widget, advance(1))
should_show_no_tooltip()
    (a dialog, a menu and a bottom sheet ARE widgets: on_tag and on_text find what is in them)

    WHAT IS PAINTED - a watched test only (-watch, or watch_ui_tests()):

    should_have_color(gui.rgb(230, 90, 90))         the colour painted in the middle of it
should_have_color_at(4, 4, colour)              ... 4 pixels in from its top left corner
color_at(4, 4)                                  the colour there, to look at yourself

    A test that is not watched paints nothing, so there is no colour to check: these words then stop the test with an illegal_state_exception that says so. They do not pass quietly, and they are not skipped.

    (They are named should_... because they are checks. The words that only ASK - exists(), is_shown(), words() in finders.sharp - answer and never fail.)
